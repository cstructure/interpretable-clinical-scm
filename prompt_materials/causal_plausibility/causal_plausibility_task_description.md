## Task: Causal Plausibility Assessment of COVID-19 Treatment Relationships

You will evaluate proposed causal relationships between patient characteristics, treatments, and outcomes from COVID-19 causal inference models. Each relationship is represented as an "edge" connecting two variables “SourceNode” → “TargetNode” (e.g., "Age → Mortality" or "Steroid Treatment → Survival").

Your Role: Using your understanding of COVID-19 pathophysiology, pharmacology, and clinical management, assess whether each proposed causal relationship is biologically and clinically plausible.
Evaluation Criteria:
* Biological mechanism: Is there a known physiological pathway supporting this relationship?
* Temporal sequence: Does the proposed cause precede the effect in a clinically realistic timeframe?
* Clinical evidence: Does this align with published research and clinical guidelines?
* Confounding considerations: Could this relationship be explained by other factors?

Rating Scale:
* 3: High: Strong biological basis and clinical evidence
* 2: Moderate: Plausible mechanism with some supporting evidence
* 1: Low: Weak or questionable biological rationale
* 0: None: No plausible causal pathway

Examples:
* "Age → Mortality": 3: High (well-established risk factor)
* "BMI → Oxygen Saturation": 3: High (obesity affects respiratory mechanics)
* "Steroid Treatment → Survival": 3: Moderate (evidence-based but context-dependent)
* "BMI → Age": 0: None (directionally implausible)
